Welcome to Weaver's Canvas

Key Features:

  • Controls Panel: Located on the left, this panel contains all settings for pattern structure, dimensions, and color.
  • Visualization Area: The main canvas on the right displays the pattern. Interact by dragging to pan and scrolling to zoom.
  • Generate Random: This button randomizes all settings to generate a new pattern.
  • Download PNG: Saves a high-resolution image of the current pattern.

All settings are organized within the accordion sections in the controls panel.

How to Use:

  • Open Controls: Tap the icon in the top-left to access the controls panel.
  • Adjust Settings: The panel contains all settings for pattern structure, dimensions, and color.
  • Interact with Pattern: Use standard touch gestures to interact. Pinch to zoom and drag with one finger to pan.
  • Generate & Download: These functions are located at the bottom of the controls panel.
